About RehoSpace Enterprise

A Tanzanian software company in Morogoro. We build products and systems that give people and businesses room to grow.

Our Story

Why we are called RehoSpace

The name comes from Rehoboth in Genesis 26:22. After seasons of quarrels, Isaac dug a well that no one fought over and said: "Now the Lord has given us room, and we will flourish in the land."

Many businesses here work hard but are held back by notebooks, lost stock and unpaid debts. We build software that clears those out of the way. We started with one product, PillPointOne, proved it with real pharmacies, and grew from there.

"Technology should create room: for businesses to run well, for teams to focus on meaningful work, and for people to grow."
