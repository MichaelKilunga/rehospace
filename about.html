About RehoSpace Enterprise

A technology company building digital products and intelligent business systems that give people and organisations room to grow.

Our Story

Why we are called RehoSpace

The name comes from Rehoboth in Genesis 26:22. After seasons of dispute and limitation, Isaac dug a well that no one contested and said: "Now the Lord has given us room, and we will flourish in the land."

That is the idea behind everything we build. Many businesses in Tanzania work hard but are held back by manual records, lost stock, unpaid debts, and systems that were never designed for them. Our job is to remove that friction and create space to grow.

RehoSpace started with one product, PillPointOne, built for pharmacies and proven with real customers before we added anything else. Today we run several live systems and build custom solutions for organisations across the country, from our base in Morogoro.

"Technology should do more than digitise existing processes. It should create room: for businesses to operate efficiently, for teams to focus on meaningful work, and for people and organisations to grow."

Built for Africa, Designed for Growth

Our long-term perspective

Africa has a rapidly growing digital economy, yet many organisations still run on fragmented, manual processes. We believe there is an enormous opportunity to build technology that understands the realities of African businesses and institutions.

Our ambition is not limited to individual client projects. We aim to build products that scale across Tanzania, East Africa and beyond, while mentoring young technology professionals and creating employment along the way.

  • Horizon 1: scale our live products and establish sustainable recurring revenue.
  • Horizon 2: build a professional engineering team and develop additional products.
  • Horizon 3: expand regionally and support community development initiatives.
