  • Stock balance, batches and expiry alerts
  • Low-stock notifications and reorder lists
  • Multi-branch stock transfers
  • Purchases and supplier records
  • Debts and installment payments
  • Mobile money payments
  • Profit and loss reports (PDF & Excel)
  • Audit trail and staff permissions
  • WhatsApp and SMS notifications

  • Daily profit and expense tracking

Communication & Messaging

SkyPush (Push SMS)

Tuma SMS kwa watu wengi papo hapo kwa sekunde chache. Reach every customer, parent or member on the phone they already carry.

SkyPush is our bulk SMS platform for businesses, schools, churches and institutions in Tanzania. Register, load credit with mobile money and send to thousands of numbers in seconds, on every Tanzanian network. It also powers the notifications inside PillPointOne, DaycareMIS and RehoPOS.

  • Instant delivery on all Tanzanian networks
  • Your business name as sender ID
  • Schedule messages in advance
  • Delivery reports and message history
  • Pay with M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa, Airtel Money, Halopesa or TTCL
  • Simple three-step process: register, load, send
  • API integration for your own software
  • Support on WhatsApp

Customer Retention & Installments

RehoCare

Getting a new customer is not enough. You need to bring them back and collect what is owed.

RehoCare is our enterprise platform for customer retention and installment management. Many land, housing and health transactions in Tanzania are paid in installments and tracked by hand, leading to disputes, double allocation and lost revenue. RehoCare solves this.

  • Automated payment schedules and balances
  • Customer accounts and payment history
  • Plot and property allocation records
  • "Withhold lock" to prevent double allocation
  • M-Pesa, Airtel Money and Mixx by Yas reconciliation
  • Offline-first for unstable connections
  • Reminders, follow-ups and retention campaigns
  • Reporting, workflows and document management
