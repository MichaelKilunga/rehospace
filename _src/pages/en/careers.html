Why We Train

Building Africa's next generation of software engineers

Every year thousands of young people in Tanzania finish college with a certificate but little real experience, and employers ask for experience first. We close that gap by putting learners to work on live software, next to engineers who build and run it every day.

We are building RehoSpace for the long term, and we want our team to grow from the people we train. Learn with us, prove yourself on real projects, and you are first in line when we hire.

Our Programmes

Four ways to start your career in software

Whether you are a complete beginner, a student who needs field attachment, or a graduate looking for experience, there is a place to start.

Software Development Course

A structured, hands-on course that takes you from the basics to building and deploying a working web application, using the same tools we use on our live products.

  • HTML, CSS and JavaScript
  • PHP, Laravel and MySQL databases
  • APIs, mobile money and SMS integrations
  • Git, teamwork and deploying to the cloud

Internships & Field Attachment

For university and college students who need practical training (field attachment), and graduates who want real experience before their first job.

  • Work on PillPointOne, DaycareMIS, RehoPOS and client projects
  • Real tasks from a real development backlog
  • An engineer as your supervisor
  • Supervision and assessment for your college or university

Mentorship

Guidance from working engineers, whether you are just starting out or already writing code on your own.

  • One-to-one sessions and code reviews
  • Help building a portfolio of real projects
  • Career advice: CVs, interviews and freelancing
  • Learning plans for self-taught developers

Careers at RehoSpace

Questions

Before you apply

Do you offer software development training in Tanzania?

Yes. RehoSpace Enterprise runs hands-on software development training from Morogoro, onsite and online, for young people across Tanzania and learners in other African countries.

Do you accept field attachment and internship students?

Yes. University and college students can do their field attachment (practical training) with us, and graduates can join as interns. You work on our live products with an engineer as your supervisor.

Is the training free?

Some programmes and intakes are free and others carry a fee. We tell you the exact cost, if any, before you join.

Do I need programming experience?

Not for the course, which starts from the basics. For internships and mentorship, tell us what you already know and we will suggest where to start.

Can I join from outside Tanzania?

Yes. Learners and interns from other African countries can join online. We teach in English and Kiswahili.

Will I get a job after training?

We cannot promise a job to everyone. When RehoSpace hires, we look first at the people we have trained, and we help every trainee build the portfolio employers ask for.

Can I earn money while I learn?

Yes, through the PillPointOne agent programme: register pharmacies and earn 20% of every PillPointOne plan payment they make. Agents are paid by commission, not salary.

Where does the training take place?

At our office in Kihonda Mizani, behind Pesame Park, Morogoro, and online.
