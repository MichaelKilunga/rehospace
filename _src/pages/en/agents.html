Questions

Before you apply

Do I pay anything to become an agent?

No. Applying and becoming an agent is free.

Is this a salaried job?

No. Agents are independent and earn commission on the pharmacies they bring. There is no fixed salary, and you choose your own hours.

Do I need sales experience?

No. You need to be honest, organised and willing to visit pharmacies. We share posters, messages and tips, and our team helps you on WhatsApp.

How does a pharmacy become my client?

The pharmacy owner signs up with your referral link or agent code, or you register the pharmacy and its branches from your dashboard.

Does the 20% apply to every sale?

It applies to every payment for a PillPointOne plan sold at our standard price. If you sell your own packages at your own price, you earn everything above our price instead of the 20%.

When and how am I paid?

Your commission lands in your wallet when your client pays. It becomes withdrawable at the end of that month, and you can withdraw from TZS 10,000 to M-Pesa or a bank account.

Where can I work?

Anywhere in Tanzania: in your neighbourhood, your town or across your region.

Where are the full terms?

The full Agent Agreement is on pillpointone.com. You read and accept it before you submit your application.
