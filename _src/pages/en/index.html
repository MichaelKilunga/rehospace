About RehoSpace

Creating digital space for people and businesses to grow

RehoSpace Enterprise is a technology company based in Morogoro, Tanzania. We combine software engineering, business process understanding, automation and data to turn everyday operational challenges into reliable digital solutions.

Our name comes from Rehoboth: a place where room was made to grow after seasons of limitation. That is what our technology is for. It removes friction so organisations can focus on serving their customers.
