Questions

Working with RehoSpace

Do you build custom software for businesses in Tanzania?

Yes. We design and build custom software, business management systems, web applications and Android apps for businesses and organisations across Tanzania, from requirements to deployment and support.

Do you work with clients outside Tanzania?

Yes. We are based in Morogoro and work remotely with clients in other African countries.

Can you integrate M-Pesa, Airtel Money and Mixx by Yas?

Yes. We connect systems to mobile money, payment gateways, SMS, WhatsApp, email and third-party APIs.

How much does a custom system cost?

It depends on what the system needs to do. Tell us about your challenge and we will send a practical proposal with a clear quotation.

Do you support the system after launch?

Yes. We train your staff, migrate your data and support you on WhatsApp and phone, with maintenance, updates and improvements.

Do you offer software development training?

Yes. We run hands-on software development courses, internships, field attachment and mentorship for young people. See Training & Careers.
